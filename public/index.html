One team

Agents and humans work side by side, under the same access control and the same rules. No separate AI corner of the business.

Hire, retire

Bring an agent in for the work you need, stand it down when you don't. The shape of the team keeps changing, and that's the point.

Costed, transparent

Every initiative, task and execution shows what it cost and what happened. Not a report you request; how the thing works.

Four kinds of work

Most of what a business actually wants falls into one of these, and Helix treats them as genuinely different sorts of work rather than all as prompts.

01 / THINK

Think

The work where the output is a judgement or an analysis rather than a thing. The type most tools skip straight past.

02 / CREATE

Create

Making the thing: the document, the design, the content, the deliverable.

03 / BUILD

Build

Building what runs afterwards, rather than producing a one-off output.

04 / AUTOMATE

Automate

The work that should stop needing a person at all, once it's proven. Decided on evidence, because every execution is costed.

Any rigour

From very little to regulated, whatever the business needs. Frameworks are yours to define, and the evidence is produced as the work happens.

Two hierarchies

What the business is, and what it is doing about it: your products and services on one side, initiatives down to a single execution on the other.

Three ways in

Voice, plain language, or point and click, from the moment you sign up. None of the three is the second-class option.

Who is Helix for?

Two doors into the same system. One for people who want the work done, one for people who build.

For operators

People who want the work done and don't want to run infrastructure. Describe the work by voice, in plain language or by pointing and clicking; hire agents for the parts worth delegating; keep the parts worth a person's judgement.

For builders

Developers who want to reuse the platform's capability inside their own agentic workloads:

  • Cortex memory, sovereign and self-hosted
  • Skills created in your workflows, reusable everywhere
  • A rules engine that makes agents deterministic where it matters
  • Generative UI: the interface builds itself around the task
  • A workflow builder, to set out how the work should run
  • Voice as a first-class interface across all of it

Privacy notice

A company selling sovereignty does not trade your details. Short, in plain words.

What we collect. Name, work email, company (optional), which products interest you, and anything you write about your use case. Signing up through the chat collects the same details, nothing more.

Why. To email you about early access to the products you chose. That is the whole purpose. The lawful basis is your consent, given by the checkbox on the form.

Where it lives. On infrastructure MindLynx controls. It is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used to train anyone's model.

How long. Until the products reach general release, or 12 months after we last hear from you, whichever comes sooner. Then it is deleted.

Your rights. Email hello@helix.work with "remove me" and your record is deleted. You can ask what we hold about you the same way. No cookies are set on this page.
