How this is built

Official sources only. Published government and regulator APIs and the publishers’ own syndication feeds. Nothing is scraped. Each source records its own licence, and these entries come from sources licensed as: No re-use licence stated by the publisher (© Housing Ombudsman Service); Open Government Licence v3.0; Open Government Licence, version not stated by the publisher.

Every line is citable. Each entry has a permanent address and a reference. Quote the reference and the link resolves to the entry, the source and the date it was read.

Change is the signal. Every register is snapshotted on every run, so what changed between two readings can be published rather than the register itself. Reading the store as at 08:43 on 20 August 2026, 6 registers had been read twice, the widest gap between two readings being 13 hours. No record moved: the registers stood still between the two readings, which is a finding rather than an absence. A register read for the first time is never reported as a register full of new records.

What we do not do. No severity score, no ranking, no index number. A single figure over events this different would be invented, and it would be quoted as though it were not. No entry characterises an organisation in words its source did not use.

This issue is capped. This feed read 500 Find a Tender releases across 5 pages of the window, oldest first, and kept the 3 that are new notices classified by the buyer under CPV division 85 (health and social work), 09 (fuel and electricity) or 65 (public utilities); notices in every other division are not read, and the window held further pages this run did not follow.
